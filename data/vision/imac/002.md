# Progress toward carbon neutral

We’ve reduced emissions for iMac (two ports) with 256GB by more than 20 percent against our business-as-usual scenario as modeled by Apple.[^7] This device contains more than 15 percent recycled content, including 100 percent recycled aluminum in the stand, reducing total product emissions by about 13 percent. We’re also working with our suppliers to transition to 100 percent low-carbon electricity for Apple production. The low-carbon electricity solutions that suppliers have already implemented to date have reduced product emissions by 5 percent. In our carbon footprint calculations, we also account for the emissions necessary to generate low-carbon electricity, specifically to manufacture and maintain renewable energy infrastructure, like wind and solar farms.
