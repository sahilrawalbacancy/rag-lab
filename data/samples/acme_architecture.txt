ACME ROBOTICS — PLATFORM ARCHITECTURE OVERVIEW
Version 2.4 | Last updated 12 February 2026 | Owner: Platform Engineering

1. HOSTING AND REGIONS
1.1 The Acme platform runs on AWS. The primary region is eu-west-1 (Ireland),
with a warm standby in eu-central-1 (Frankfurt).
1.2 All customer data for EU tenants remains within the EU. There is no
transfer of EU customer data outside the EEA.
1.3 Compute runs on managed Kubernetes (EKS). Nodes are replaced rather than
patched in place, on a maximum 30-day image age.

2. NETWORK ARCHITECTURE
2.1 Public ingress is limited to the application load balancer and the CDN.
2.2 Databases and internal services sit in private subnets with no route to
the internet. Outbound traffic egresses through a NAT gateway with an
allow-list.
2.3 A web application firewall fronts all public endpoints, with managed
rulesets for the OWASP Top 10 plus rate limiting per source IP.

3. AVAILABILITY AND RESILIENCE
3.1 The platform targets 99.9% monthly availability, measured at the load
balancer.
3.2 Recovery Time Objective (RTO) is 4 hours. Recovery Point Objective (RPO)
is 15 minutes.
3.3 Databases use synchronous replication within the primary region and
asynchronous replication to the standby region.
3.4 Failover to the standby region is tested twice per year as a documented
game-day exercise.

4. BACKUP
4.1 Full database snapshots are taken daily; transaction logs are shipped
continuously.
4.2 Backups are retained for 35 days. Backup restoration is tested quarterly.
4.3 Backups are stored in a separate AWS account with a distinct trust
boundary, so a compromise of the production account cannot delete them.

5. TENANCY MODEL
5.1 Acme operates a shared-infrastructure, logically-isolated multi-tenant
model. A single-tenant deployment is available on the Enterprise plan.
5.2 Every request carries an authenticated tenant context. The data-access
layer refuses any query lacking a tenant predicate.

6. THIRD-PARTY SUBPROCESSORS
6.1 Acme uses AWS (hosting), Stripe (payments), SendGrid (transactional
email), and Datadog (observability).
6.2 A current subprocessor list is published at acme.example/subprocessors and
customers are notified 30 days before a new subprocessor is engaged.
